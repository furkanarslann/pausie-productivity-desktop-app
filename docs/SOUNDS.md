composition and the recording. Each file was checked on its Wikimedia Commons page on 2026-11-13.

Edits: the clip starts where the music starts, is 55 s long with a 0.3 s fade-in and a 5 s fade-out,
and is normalized to about −18 LUFS (true peak −3 dB) so every sound plays at a similar level.

Not used, because the recording itself isn't public domain even though the music is: Kimiko Ishizaka's
FLAC upload of the same prelude (CC BY 3.0), *Clair de lune* by Laurens Goedhart (CC BY 3.0) and Satie's
*Gnossienne No. 1* by La Pianista (CC BY-SA 3.0 / GFDL).

The macOS chimes (Glass, Hero, Ping, Purr, Submarine, Tink, Bottle, Blow) are not bundled: the app plays
the system's own sounds from `/System/Library/Sounds`.
